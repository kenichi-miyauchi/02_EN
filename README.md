# 02 Rebooting the Ocean Ecosystem

## A Concept Proposal for Integrated Microplastic Removal and Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement

### 1. Introduction

An integrated approach to marine environmental recovery, combining marine microplastic removal, ocean alkalinity enhancement, and land-based TRWP reduction.

### 2. Removal of Marine Plastic

- Collect microplastics from ocean surface and shallow layers using specialized vessels (MA Vessels).
- Apply ultrafiltration and electrocoagulation.
- Enhance treated seawater alkalinity through controlled lime addition.
- Use aerial, surface, and underwater drones for ocean observation and environmental monitoring.

### 3. Reduction of TRWP

- Capture TRWP from vehicles and road surfaces.
- Introduce roadside filtration measures to prevent TRWP from entering rivers and the ocean.

### 4. Summary

This concept combines marine recovery measures with land-based pollution prevention to address multiple environmental challenges.

It aims to support marine environmental recovery and investigate the potential contribution of ocean alkalinity enhancement to the ocean's natural carbon uptake capacity.

Scientific validation, pilot testing, and environmental impact assessment are required to establish technical feasibility and effectiveness.
